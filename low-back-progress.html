اختبارات أسفل الظهر | Low Back Functional Tests

Pain Level
Pain scale 0-10. Lower score is better. Use current pain or pain during main functional activity.
—
Lumbar Flexion ROM
Assess forward bending tolerance. Use percentage of expected movement or fingertip-to-floor score converted clinically.
—
Lumbar Extension ROM
Assess backward bending tolerance and symptom response. Enter clinical score from 0-100%.
—
Sit to Stand
Count controlled sit-to-stand repetitions in 30 seconds, or enter functional tolerance score based on clinic standard.
—
Plank Hold
Measure hold time in seconds. Assesses trunk endurance and symptom tolerance.
—
Single Leg Balance
Measure best single-leg balance time in seconds. Useful for control and confidence.
—
Walking Tolerance
Enter walking tolerance score 0-100% based on symptoms, endurance, and functional limitation.
—
Functional Movement Quality
Observe bending, lifting, transitions, fear avoidance, guarding, compensation, and movement confidence.
Clinical Observation
Patient Confidence
Patient-reported confidence during daily activity. 0 = no confidence, 10 = full confidence.
